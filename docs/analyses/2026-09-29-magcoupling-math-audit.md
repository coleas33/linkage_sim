All 46 failing checks (32 root-cause groups) are classified. One further group, the onset-calibration sensitivity check, was a check bug; it was fixed and now passes (see the rulings).

Headline effect at the default design:

- **E1 (adhesive shear modulus):** Temperature design!C106 changes from "Above the lap-shear strength at the block ends" to "Below the lap-shear strength", and C202 from "Above the fatigue endurance: qualify by thermal cycling" to "Below the fatigue endurance".
- **E2 (clamp screw length):** the recommended screw changes from "ISO 4762 M4 x 12, class 12.9" to "ISO 4762 M4 x 14, class 12.9", and that screw protrudes 0.34 mm from the 25 mm boss. No standard M4 length both engages 2 × d of thread and stays inside the boss.
- **E3 (library remanence):** at the vendor's minimum Br of 1.30 T the adhesive fatigue screen reads "OK: 7x margin" (was 8x) and the 1.25 mm row of the gap sweep reads "nominal: test needed" (was "below hot minimum"). From Br = 1.3025 T up, the clamp recommendation becomes "None: enlarge the boss or the clamp length".
- **E4 (pole-sweep hub wall):** the 6-pole row of the pole sweep changes from "below hot minimum" to "outside OD envelope".
- **E5 (rear-web eddy loss):** no text changes, but the high-case steady magnet temperature rises from 89.77 °C to 92.51 °C, 0.04 °C below the 92.55 °C magnet limit.
- **M1 (cup back-iron requirement, model approximation):** if corrected, Materials!C22 would advise a corner wall of at least 2.2 mm instead of 2.0 mm.
- No finding changes the hot-torque verdicts: "Nominal only: hot test" on the Calculator and "Below hot minimum" on Metal design both stay.
